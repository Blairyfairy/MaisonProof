At a glance

This page presents the repository's documented plans and records. A documented requirement or proposed milestone is not evidence that it has been deployed or independently verified.

Release gate evidence

Operational acceptance checklist — these are verification requirements, not a claim that each activity has been executed.

  • 01Verify approved scope, ownership and rollback plan
  • 02Run functional, security, accessibility and regression checks
  • 03Confirm monitoring, alerts, support and operational readiness
  • 04Record sign-off and unresolved exceptions
  • 05Retain deployment and post-release validation evidence

Completion standard: link the corresponding implementation, test result, decision record or stakeholder approval before marking an item complete.

Release and go/no-go checklist

  • Legal entity, domain and trademark clearance confirmed
  • Qualified authentication personnel and published SOP operating
  • Secure reviewer permissions, second approval for high-risk cases
  • Signed item-bound certificate, live public lookup, revocation tested
  • Seller cannot self-certify or override badge
  • Shipping and insurance partners contracted; accurate terms displayed
  • Recording consent, retention, privacy notices and deletion procedures approved
  • Payment, refunds, disputes, tax and billing tested
  • Platform policy and app reviews completed where required
  • Pen test, accessibility audit, load tests, restore drill passed
  • Monitoring, incident/on-call, support escalation and SLO dashboard staffed
  • Merchant pilot feedback and unit economics reviewed
  • Founder/Product/Trust/Security/Legal/Ops sign go/no-go decision Rollback: disable badge rendering or revoke affected certificates, roll back adapters, preserve audit logs, notify affected merchants.
