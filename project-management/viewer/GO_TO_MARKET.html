Go-to-market plan

Beachhead: independent U.S. luxury accessories resellers with existing WooCommerce shops and small high-value catalogs. Avoid promising every luxury category at launch. Positioning: independent item-specific authentication that follows the merchant's own storefront; not a marketplace and not manufacturer endorsement. Acquisition: direct outreach to specialty merchants, industry events, vetted partners, case studies from consented pilots, platform extension directories after approvals. Sales: discovery → risk/ROI estimate → pilot contract → merchant onboarding → 5–20 reviews → results review → subscription expansion. Claims policy: no unverified trust metrics, manufacturer endorsements, insurance guarantees or accuracy percentages. Pricing tests: per-case only vs subscription + case, turnaround tiers, enterprise volume; measure contribution margin and retention.
