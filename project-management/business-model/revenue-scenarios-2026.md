# MaisonProof — Revenue architecture and illustrative earnings scenarios

**November 22, 2026 · Concept-stage planning model, not a market forecast.** This document supplements the existing Lean Canvas and does not replace it. No market share, revenue, authentication accuracy, or product inventory has been validated.

## Monetization lanes

1. Physical inspection fees: illustrative $175–$350 per submitted item, excluding insured logistics.
2. Guided remote triage: illustrative $55–$125; no conclusive approval without sufficient evidence.
3. Merchant plans: illustrative $149–$999/month for workflow, API access and support, with usage fees separate.
4. Enterprise integrations: negotiated platform and onboarding fees; no assumed signed customers.
5. Logistics handling: only actual earned margin, after carrier/insurance cost, counted as revenue.
6. Reinspection, expedited service and appeals: optional, subject to fair process and conflict controls.

**Important:** Luxury product listing prices are NOT MaisonProof revenue. Gross merchandise value (GMV) is not authentication-service revenue. The sample product statuses do not represent actual inspections.

## Operating discipline and milestones

- **Evidence integrity:** unique item ID, intake photos, chain of custody, tamper evidence, audit trail, signed reviewer decision and dispute procedure.
- **Quality:** false-approval/false-rejection reviews, blinded secondary checks, brand/model specialist routing and liability coverage.
- **Customer acquisition:** pilot with 3–5 independent resellers; measure conversion, retention, contribution per case and actual turnaround times before scaling.
- **Commercial risk:** product manufacturer price is not resale value; avoid presenting third-party brand names as partnerships or endorsement.
- **Decision gates:** scale only after measurable inspection accuracy, defensible unit economics, insurance feasibility, privacy/security controls and repeat demand.
