# Product strategy and North Star
**Vision:** Make a luxury resale item’s authentication decision understandable, auditable, and revocable at the point of sale.

**Initial beachhead (hypothesis):** independent US luxury resale merchants handling handbags and watches, with WooCommerce-first integration and both shipment-based inspection and remote triage. Do not treat remote triage as equivalent to physical authentication.

**Jobs to be done:** Merchants need defensible, item-level decisions and low-friction commerce badges; buyers need a trustworthy, readable proof record; reviewers need evidence integrity and escalation; operations need traceable custody and disputes.

**North Star proposal:** number of *valid, item-bound proof records viewed by unique buyers* per month, with quality guardrails. Avoid optimizing for approvals or revenue alone.

**Strategic pillars:** (1) independent review quality, (2) trustworthy public record, (3) merchant time-to-value, (4) viable contribution margin, (5) transparent limits and revocation.

**Non-goals for v1:** claiming brand affiliation, automatic AI-only approvals, offering insurance without contracts, universal luxury categories, and public disclosure of sensitive evidence.

**Success gates:** validated willingness to pay; acceptable independently reviewed false-approval rate; provable chain of custody; viable per-case contribution; repeat usage by pilot merchants. Targets are proposed and must be agreed before launch.
