# Positioning and messaging
**For** independent luxury resale sellers **who** need credible evidence for item-level trust, **MaisonProof** is a third-party authentication workflow and public-record layer **that** documents the scope, method, status, and revocation of a review. **Unlike** self-issued seller badges, it separates the merchant from the final review decision. Differentiation is a hypothesis pending competitor research.

**Message hierarchy:** independent decision → documented method and limitations → item-bound public record → status checked at display time → optional commerce integration.

**Claims policy:** Use 'demo verified' only for simulated records. Do not say 'guaranteed authentic', 'insured', 'certified by brand', or 'fraud-proof' without substantiation and approval. Product photographs, prices, and manufacturer trademarks are illustrative references, not partnerships.

**Objection handling:** 'Can remote verify?' → only where evidence and policy allow; otherwise escalate. 'What if the decision changes?' → revocation/dispute workflow. 'Who pays shipping?' → quote explicitly, after carrier agreements. 'Is this affiliated with Gucci/Rolex/etc.?' → no such relationship is established.
