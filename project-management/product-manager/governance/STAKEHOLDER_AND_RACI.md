# Governance and RACI
Roles to assign: Product (P), Engineering (E), Design (D), Authentication Policy (A), Security/Privacy (S), Legal (L), Operations (O), Finance (F), Growth (G), Executive Sponsor (X).

| Decision | Accountable | Consulted | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product scope/priorities | P | E,D,A,G | PRD, research |
| Authenticity policy | A | P,S,L | SOP, reviewer QA |
| Badge safety | S | A,E,L,P | Threat tests |
| Pricing | F | P,G,O | Paid pilot economics |
| Public claims | L | P,G,A | Claims register |
| Production launch | X | All | Go/no-go signed record |

Maintain a decision log with date, alternatives, evidence IDs, risks, owner and revisit date. Any unassigned accountable role blocks the associated decision.
