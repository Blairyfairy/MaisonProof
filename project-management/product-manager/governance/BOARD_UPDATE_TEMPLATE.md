# Monthly product and investment review
**Reporting period:** [date] | **Owner:** [name] | **Decision required:** [yes/no]

1. Executive summary: what changed, evidence, decision.
2. North Star and funnel: actual, baseline, variance, source.
3. Trust and quality: decisions, disagreements, disputes, revocations, incidents.
4. Delivery: completed milestones, blockers, burn rate, scope changes.
5. Commercial: merchant cohorts, actual pricing, gross margin, CAC, cash runway.
6. Risks and compliance: critical open controls and owner.
7. Customer insights: verbatim sourced quotes with consent, disconfirming evidence.
8. Decisions and asks: recommendation, alternatives, threshold citations.

No blank field should be interpreted as achieved. Attach raw metric and evidence links.
