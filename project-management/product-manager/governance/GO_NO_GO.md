# Executive launch decision and evidence thresholds

| Gate | Required evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|

| G2 Decision quality | Expert policy, blind QA, threshold signed by risk owner | Pending |
| G3 No false verified states | End-to-end tests incl. revocation, cache, item mismatch | Pending |
| G4 Unit economics | Observed costs and positive path to contribution | Pending |
| G5 Legal and consumer claims | Counsel approval, rights audit, disclosures | Pending |
| G6 Insurance/logistics | Executed carrier and coverage agreements | Pending |
| G7 Security/privacy | Threat model, privacy review, pen test, response drill | Pending |
| G8 Platform compliance | Integration review and deployment readiness | Pending |
| G9 Operational support | Staffed support, appeals, escalation, SLAs | Pending |

**Decision options:** proceed to discovery; run closed pilot once minimum controls pass; redesign; delay; stop. No production launch until all critical gates pass and accountable approvers sign with evidence IDs.
