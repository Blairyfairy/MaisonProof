# Customer discovery — consented research protocol
**Recruit:** 12–20 independent merchants, 6–10 buyers, 4–6 qualified authenticators, and carrier/insurance specialists; these are research targets, not completed interviews.

**Consent script:** Explain purpose, recording choice, retention, confidentiality, withdrawal and incentives. Obtain affirmative consent before recording; store consent separately from transcripts.

**Merchant questions:** Walk through your last disputed sale; show how item identity is established; what proof is currently shared; how many items/month need review; what causes delay; what do you pay now; what is the biggest risk; what would prevent adoption; would you choose remote or physical and why; test pricing only after describing existing behavior.

**Buyer questions:** What would convince you a certificate is real? What status, scope, date and revocation details do you expect? What would you do if status changed? Test unverified vs demo-verified badge comprehension.

**Reviewer questions:** Which evidence is necessary per category? What triggers second review or escalation? How do you detect duplicate cases? How is a false approval investigated?

**Synthesis:** Record exact participant quotes only from actual sessions; label interpretations and hypotheses separately; seek disconfirming evidence; publish a de-identified thematic analysis and decision impact.
