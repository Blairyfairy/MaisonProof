# Competitive research matrix
Research real providers by category, geography, review method, evidence burden, advertised fee, turnaround, public verification, revocation, integrations, dispute terms, and liability. Competitor candidates to verify include Entrupy, Real Authentication, LegitGrails, and platform-native marketplace programs; these are research leads, not a verified current comparison.

| Dimension | MaisonProof target | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Decision independence | Separate merchant and reviewer | Governance design and audit |
| Method disclosure | Remote vs physical stated | Sample certificates |
| Revocation | Immediate invalidation | End-to-end tests |
| Commerce distribution | WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento | Platform compliance |
| Unit economics | Positive contribution per case | Pilot cost data |

**Decision:** Do not claim a competitive moat until evidence supports meaningful differentiation and willingness to pay.
