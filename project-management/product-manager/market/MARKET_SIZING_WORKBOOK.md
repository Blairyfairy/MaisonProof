# TAM / SAM / SOM research specification
**Do not insert invented market values.** Size two separate markets: authentication services revenue and resale merchandise GMV; never count resale GMV as MaisonProof revenue.

**Bottom-up model:** SAM = eligible active US luxury-resale merchants × eligible items per merchant per month × paid review adoption × average net authentication fee × 12 + addressable subscription spend. SOM = reachable merchants × activated share × reviews per activated merchant × net fee × 12 + realized subscription revenue.

**Evidence to collect:** number of merchants by platform and geography; relevant categories; volume distribution; existing authentication workflow; inspection fee benchmarks; real conversion; churn; category-specific review limits; carrier costs; payment fees; competitor coverage and differentiation.

**Source log fields:** metric, value, geography, period, primary URL, publisher, retrieval date, methodology, confidence, contradiction, owner. Prefer public filings, platform disclosures, official price pages, and primary merchant interviews. Distinguish point estimates from ranges.

**Scenario:** build low/base/high ranges for each assumption; use sensitivity tornado chart; do not present unsupported precision as market fact. Update at least quarterly.
