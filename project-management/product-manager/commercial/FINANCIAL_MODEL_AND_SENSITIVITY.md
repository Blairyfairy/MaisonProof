# Financial model — reconciliation and sensitivities
**Baseline scenario (USD/month, illustrative; copied conceptually from existing planning model):**

| Case | Inspections | Net fee | Accounts | Subscription | Variable/case | Fixed costs | Revenue | Contribution |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 100 | $135 | 5 | $149 | $80 | $18,000 | $14,245 | -$11,755 |
| Base | 500 | $155 | 30 | $249 | $75 | $55,000 | $84,970 | -$7,530 |
| Upside | 1,500 | $175 | 100 | $349 | $70 | $130,000 | $297,400 | $62,400 |

Revenue = inspections × net fee + accounts × subscription; contribution = revenue − inspections × variable cost − fixed costs. Simplified, excludes unmodeled overhead and taxes.

**Break-even inspection volume** at fixed account count = (fixed costs − subscription revenue) / (net fee − variable cost), rounded up where margin positive. Conservative ≈314; base ≈1,137; upside ≈1,359. These are *mathematical implications of assumptions*, not forecasts.

**Stress tests:** 20% fewer inspections, 25% higher expert labor, 3% dispute reserve, 10% refund rate, 2-month merchant acquisition delay, higher insurer deductibles, slower receivables, channel platform restrictions. Update actuals monthly; record forecast error and assumptions provenance.
