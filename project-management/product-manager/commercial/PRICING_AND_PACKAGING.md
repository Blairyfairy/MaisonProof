# Pricing and packaging decision memo
**Hypotheses only:** remote triage $55–$125; physical $175–$350; merchant subscriptions $149–$999/month; enterprise integration negotiated. Existing `business-model/revenue-scenarios-2026.md` remains the source of the baseline scenario and is not overwritten.

**Package proposals:** Starter (pay-as-you-go), Merchant (volume pricing + workflow), Enterprise (SLA/API/onboarding). Each proposal needs cost-to-serve, refund policy, scope, liability, turnaround target, and explicit shipping/insurance terms.

**Price tests:** Van Westendorp as directional only; actual paid pilot/contract conversion stronger evidence; randomized offers only where fair and lawful. Analyze willingness-to-pay by category and method, not aggregate GMV. Validate margin after expert time, payment processing, evidence storage, dispute reserves, refunds, insurance and shipping subsidies.

**Guardrail:** A luxury item's listing price is not MaisonProof revenue; no claim of commissions or brand partnerships without agreements.
