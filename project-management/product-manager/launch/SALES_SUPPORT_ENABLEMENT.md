# Commercial and customer enablement
**Sales kit:** problem one-pager, truthful positioning, scope and limitations, pilot proposal, pricing sheet marked hypothetical until approved, sample certificate clearly marked DEMO, objection FAQ, merchant onboarding checklist, security questionnaire and partner integration guide.

**Customer lifecycle:** qualification → contract/consent → merchant identity → test item → first review → badge integration → buyer-record comprehension → support and renewal. Assign accountable owner and success milestone to each stage.

**Support runbooks:** no false-positive approvals; evidence upload failure; shipment loss; status dispute; expired/revoked certificate; payment refund; account compromise; accessibility complaint. Escalate legal/security incidents immediately under approved incident policy.
