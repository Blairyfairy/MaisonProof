# Pilot and launch plan
**Pre-pilot:** sign pilot agreements; confirm legal wording, reviewer policy, payment flow, privacy, custody insurance, support escalation and incident response; ensure demo badges cannot masquerade as real.

**Pilot scope proposal:** 3–5 consenting merchants, restricted categories, capped volume, explicit service limitations, human review, defined refund and appeal paths. This is a target, not recruited customers.

**Pilot measurement:** actual paid conversion, first-value time, average review labor, rework, disagreement, disputed decisions, fulfillment exceptions, record views, margin per case, qualitative trust comprehension and merchant retention.

**Launch sequence:** internal dogfood → closed pilot → monitored beta → go/no-go review → controlled general availability. Marketing must not announce brand partnerships, guarantees or certified inventory without proof.

**Rollback triggers:** false verified badge, signing compromise, material privacy incident, unsafe shipping, excessive dispute rate, unprofitable economics beyond approved tolerance.
