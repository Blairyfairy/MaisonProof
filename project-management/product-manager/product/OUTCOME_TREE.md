# Opportunity solution tree
**Desired outcome:** trusted, profitable, item-bound verification at checkout.

**Opportunities:** buyer confusion about provenance; merchant manual work; costly review logistics; evidence gaps; public record forgery; uncertain trust signal; disputes and revocation latency.

**Candidate solutions:** public record QR/deep link; signed decision token; item binding and photo fingerprint; reviewer console; remote evidence intake; physical custody tracking; commerce plugins; badge state design; disputes dashboard.

**Experiments:** comprehension tests for badges; concierge merchant pilot; reviewer time study; signed-link tamper tests; fee willingness-to-pay interviews; conversion A/B test only after real consent and sufficient power.

**Decision principle:** prioritize validated customer problems, not design polish or demo approval count.
