# Content design and accessibility
Use readable labels 'Demo verified — not a real authentication' and 'Not verified' with text plus shape/icon; never rely on green/red alone. Keep the gold serif M as the brand symbol, not the approval symbol. All badges need accessible names and links to distinct record IDs. Contrast minimum WCAG AA; keyboard focus, mobile touch targets, screen-reader announcements for status changes, and plain-language scope/limitations. All product imagery needs descriptive alt text and a source/license audit. Confirm manufacturer trademarks and commercial imagery permissions before public launch.
