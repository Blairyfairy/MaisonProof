# Agile delivery cadence
Weekly: discovery synthesis; backlog refinement; engineering/design sync; risk and dependency review; instrumentation check. Biweekly: sprint planning, demo, QA review, retrospective and stakeholder report. Monthly: product council with metrics, unit economics, decision log and roadmap adjustments. Quarterly: strategy and market refresh.

**Definition of ready:** user problem, source evidence, acceptance criteria, owner, UX, data/security implications, dependencies and test plan.
**Definition of done:** implemented, peer reviewed, automated tests, accessibility check, security/privacy approval when relevant, monitoring, docs, rollback, owner acceptance.
**Change control:** log scope changes, cost impact, schedule risk and approver; do not quietly change existing demo branding or prices.
