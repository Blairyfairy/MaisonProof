# MaisonProof — product management operating system
**Baseline:** November 22, 2026 | **Stage:** concept / demo | **Owner:** product manager (unassigned) | **Status:** planning artifacts prepared; external validation pending.

This package extends the original 100-file repository **without altering any existing file, logo, image, code, product price, badge, or Lean Canvas**. It is a decision-ready PM operating system, not evidence that research, contracts, security certification, authentication, or launch has happened.

## Product outcome
Independent, item-bound luxury authentication decisions with a public, revocable proof record and commerce integrations. The demo's green 'Demo Verified' is a simulated state, not a real determination.

## Decision policy
Never claim real inventory, verified authenticity, insurance coverage, endorsements, or realized earnings based on mockups. Actual manufacturer prices and image permissions require recheck before publication. The sample badge must never be interpreted as a real certificate.
