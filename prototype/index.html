Choose a review path

Two ways to submit an item.

01 / Insured shipment

Ship an item for physical examination, with built-in or approved third-party insurance options subject to actual partner availability.

02 / Remote review

Submit existing authentication, photographs, recorded walkthroughs, and live reviewer-requested evidence through correspondence.

Both paths require an independent MaisonProof decision. Neither shipping an item nor uploading a certificate guarantees authentication.
