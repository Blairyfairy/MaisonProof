# Commercial model (proposed, not validated prices)

**Positioning:** MAISONPROOF Verify is a provenance workflow and independent review infrastructure for luxury resale, boutiques, consignment, jewelry and collectible merchants. Do not claim manufacturer endorsement.

| Plan | Monthly price (illustrative) | Included | Overage |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Starter | $79 | 250 active SKUs, provenance badges, 1 store | $0.10/additional SKU |
| Atelier | $249 | 2,000 SKUs, 3 stores, review queue, CSV imports | $0.05/additional SKU |
| Maison | $799 | 15,000 SKUs, 10 stores, audit exports, priority support | custom |
| Enterprise | Custom | SSO, dedicated tenant, SLA, custom verification partnerships | contracted |

**Authentication review:** Charge separately for expert physical/digital authentication; for example $15–$75+ per item based on category and risk. Do not promise a fixed fee until qualified partner costs and liability are established. The base subscription is for software and provenance records, **not** guaranteed authentication.

**Illustrative SaaS revenue:** 100 Starter + 40 Atelier + 10 Maison = $25,850 MRR / $310,200 ARR gross before hosting, support, payment fees, customer acquisition, insurance, and review costs. This is a scenario, not a forecast.

**Go-to-market:** (1) validate willingness to pay with 10–20 independent luxury resale merchants; (2) pilot with 2–3 shops and independent authenticators; (3) charge for software after reliability and review workflow are tested; (4) partner with insurers, consignment operators, distributors, and commerce agencies; (5) introduce signed item passports and API access.

**Protect the brand:** Trademark clearance, domain search, registered entity, terms, DPA, and appropriate E&O/cyber coverage before selling. Open-source connectors can be MIT licensed; commercial SaaS, brand assets, and hosted verification services remain separately monetized.
